# Privacy Policy for Indicatrix

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy covers the Indicatrix desktop application ("the app"), published by
suitable-name. The app is a gemstone faceting design and rendering program. It is open source
under the MIT License; its source code is at https://github.com/suitable-name/indicatrix.

## Summary

The app does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no user accounts, no
advertising, no analytics and no telemetry. Your designs, your design library and your settings
stay on your computer. The app connects to the internet only for the two purposes described
below, and only after you allow it or set it up yourself.

## Data stored on your computer

The app saves the following in your own user folders on your computer:

- your settings and preferences;
- your design library (a local database file) and the designs, renders, cutting instructions and
  other files you save or export;
- files you open or import, which the app reads from the locations you choose.

This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by deleting those files or by
uninstalling the app.

## Network connections

**1. Checking for a new version (optional).** The app asks you whether it may check for updates.
If you allow it, it sends at most one request a day to GitHub's public API
(`api.github.com`) to read the version number of the latest Indicatrix release (and one more
whenever you press "Check Now" in Preferences). The request
contains no personal data. It carries the app's name and version (for example
`indicatrix-cut/0.7.2`), as GitHub requires. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address
to GitHub, which processes it under the GitHub Privacy Statement
(https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Nothing is
downloaded or installed automatically. You can turn the check off in Preferences at any time.

**2. Remote rendering and remote libraries (optional, set up by you).** If you connect the app
to a render coordinator or worker, the app sends that machine what it needs for the job: the
design geometry, material, lighting and camera settings. It may also read or copy design library
entries from that machine. You choose and operate these machines yourself (for example your own
computers or servers). The connections use mutually authenticated, encrypted TLS. No data goes
to us or to any third party through this feature.

If you open a link in the app (the user manual, the release page or the project website), it
opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Data we collect

None. We do not receive, store or process any personal data from your use of the app.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
